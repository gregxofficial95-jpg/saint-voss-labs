Saint Leo's Mart is a student-focused grocery ordering experience designed to make discovering products and placing an order simple, visual and fast.

02 / THE PROBLEM

TURNING A SIMPLE GROCERY SERVICE INTO A DIGITAL EXPERIENCE.

The challenge was to create a clearer and more structured way for students to discover available groceries, view pricing, select what they needed and move from browsing to ordering without unnecessary friction.

03 / THE CONCEPT

SHOP.
SELECT.
ORDER.

The concept was to build a lightweight e-commerce experience focused on the needs of students — keeping the interface visual and straightforward while connecting the ordering process directly to WhatsApp.
